⚠ This part was missing from the original BOM. Standard solenoid valves are rated 150–300 PSI — they will rupture at 28 MPa. You need a high-pressure solenoid rated ≥ 6,000 PSI (≥ 41 MPa) with a normally-closed (NC) fail-safe configuration. Confirm rating with Parker or Asco before ordering. Estimated cost: $400–600.

BOM Corrections Summary

Fix #1 Pressure transducer — changed from K4708 (1–5V DC, ~$400) to 5DEK9 (G17M0242F25000#, 4-20 mA, ~$200). K4708 has the wrong output type for the 250 Ω shunt wiring already designed. 5DEK9 cuts cost in half.
Fix #2 Relief valve — confirmed correct as Swagelok SS-RL3S4 (6,000 PSI). The previously listed Parker 442F42 is rated only 114 PSI and would rupture at operating pressure. Set pressure must be specified at 4,350 PSI (30 MPa) when ordering.
Fix #3 Vent solenoid valve (⑫) — was completely missing from original BOM. Added as required purchase. Must be rated ≥ 6,000 PSI with 24VDC NC coil. Estimated $400–600 from Parker or Asco.
